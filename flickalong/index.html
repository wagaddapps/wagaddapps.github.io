The swipe

Left is a no. Right is a yes.

One card, one film or series, one decision. Swipe left if it’s not for tonight, right if you’d watch it. Everyone decides on their own phone.

  • Already seen it? Tap the eye: it leaves your queue, and you can rate it from 1 to 10 stars.
  • Watch the trailer to decide. One tap plays it right from the card, with the summary and the rating. Changed your mind? Undo your last swipe.
  • Share it. Send the title to a best friend, or share a link that opens the app.
  • One tap further. See it in cinemas near you, or play it with Flix when it’s in your library.

Swipe together

Decide together, not alone.

Link up with your partner, a best friend or the whole family. Everyone swipes on their own phone: right if you’d watch it, left if not. When you all say yes, it’s a match.

  • Partner & best friends. Link in seconds with an invite code.
  • Group rooms. Family and friends movie nights, up to 7 people.
  • Kept for later. Likes, mutual matches and saved actors, all in your library.

Tonight’s pick

From “what should we watch?” to play.

Too many matches, or none yet? FlickAlong narrows it down for you.

  • What should we watch? Pick a style: from your likes, something new, or what you both matched.
  • Two ideas each time. One film and one series; shuffle until it feels right.
  • Just say it. “90s thriller, not too gory”: the Watch together assistant turns your mood into a swipe session.
  • Mutual Roulette & Surprise Me. Can’t decide? Let FlickAlong pick.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is FlickAlong free?

Yes. Swiping, matches, where to watch, cinema showtimes and Flix are free. Premium (Family & Friends) adds bigger group rooms, more best-friend links and unlimited group sessions.

How do we match?

Link with your partner or a best friend with an invite code from your profile, or open a group room. Swipe on your own phones: when everyone likes the same title, it appears in Matches.

What is Flix? Does FlickAlong provide films or TV?

No. FlickAlong doesn’t sell, host or provide any content. Flix is an optional player for a streaming service you already subscribe to: you enter the address and sign-in details it gave you (Profile → Streaming account), and they stay on your device.

Can I watch on my TV?

Yes, from the player: AirPlay (sound only, or picture and sound on an Apple TV or AirPlay TV), Chromecast, or screen mirroring on Android.

Can I use it with my kids?

Yes. Suggestions respect the age in your profile, and in Flix a 4-digit parental code keeps grown-up titles out of sight everywhere: lists, search, live TV and favourites.

Does it work on both my iPhone and my iPad?

Yes. Sign in on both and your resume points, lists, favourites and reminders follow your account. Your streaming sign-in details never leave your device, so you add them once on each.

Can I watch offline?

Yes, with Flix: download a film or a whole season and watch it without a connection.

Which languages is the app in?

English and French. It follows your phone’s language.

Where do cinema showtimes come from?

From cinema listings in your country. Allow location access to see the closest cinemas, or set your country in your profile.

What data do you collect?

What the app needs to work: your account, your links and your matches. Sharing usage data to help us improve is optional: you choose at first launch and can change it anytime in Profile. Everything is explained in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. More details on the Delete Account page.
