Delete your account

FlickAlong · account and data deletion

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FlickAlong and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, email info@digi4all.ai from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We verify ownership and delete within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials.
  • Your profile: display name, date of birth, country.
  • Your activity: likes, passes, matches, partner and friend links, group-room membership, and the taste profile derived from them.
  • Your push notification token.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups, overwritten on a rolling basis within 30 days.
  • Records we are required to keep by law (for example, purchase records held for tax purposes).

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings, otherwise it keeps renewing.
