This Privacy Policy explains how FlickAlong ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using FlickAlong you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (stored securely in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Profile information: your display name, date of birth, and country/region. Your date of birth is used to apply age-appropriate content filters; your country is used to show relevant streaming availability and cinemas.
  • Activity in the app: the titles you like or pass on, your matches, the partners and friends you link with, the group rooms you join, and the taste preferences derived from your activity to power recommendations.
  • Usage data, only if you accept it: when you open the app, the streaming platforms you tap, the titles you like, pass on or mark as seen, the pages you open, and what you search for. In Flix: the channels and titles you start, and how long you watch.
  • Approximate location, only with your permission: used to show cinemas and showtimes near you. You can decline; the feature falls back to the main city of the country set in your profile.
  • Device & notification data: a push notification token so we can send you notifications, and basic technical information needed to operate the app.
  • Your own streaming account, only if you add one (Flix): the server address, username and password of a streaming service you subscribe to (or an M3U link, or a portal address and a MAC address). What you type in the app is stored only in your device's secure Keychain (not backed up, not synced), is never sent to us (not even by the sync described below), and is deleted when you remove the account in the app; you add your account on each of your devices. A local index of the titles your account offers is kept on your device only. FlickAlong does not provide any streaming account or content.
  • “Add from the web”, only if you use it (Flix): so that you do not have to type on a TV remote, the app can show a MAC address and a device key that it generated for your device (not your hardware’s MAC address). Opening that screen sends us this MAC address and a one-way fingerprint of the device key. A playlist you then type on our web page (server address, username and password, or an M3U link) is stored on our servers under that MAC address so that your device can fetch it. It stays there until you remove it on the same page; the device entry and its playlists are also deleted once the device has not been used for six months. This is the only case in which streaming-account details reach our servers.
  • Sync between your devices, only if you turn it on (Flix): you are asked the first time you open Flix, and you can change your mind at any time with Flix’s “Sync between devices” setting. When it is on, your resume points (title, poster, position), your TV favourites (the lists you build, with the name and logo of each entry), the reminders you set for programmes (programme title, start and reminder times), what you recently watched live and your display settings (4K, languages) are saved to your account so they follow you from one device to another, together with the “adult” flag of the entries concerned. Each streaming account’s data is filed under a one-way fingerprint of it: nothing sent contains your server address, username, password or M3U link, and a poster or logo hosted on your server stays on your device. You add your streaming account on each of your devices; once added, it shows the same data. What your device already holds is added to your account when you turn sync on. Parental-control codes, downloads and passwords are never sent.
  • Subscription status: whether you hold an active subscription. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

FlickAlong has no photo upload, no public profile, and no private messaging.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To provide the core features: swiping, matching, linking a partner or friend, group rooms, and recommendations.
  • To show streaming availability and, where available, cinema showtimes near you.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, if you accept usage data.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To keep your Flix data in step between your devices, if you turn sync on.
  • To manage subscriptions and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. What other users can see

When you link a partner or best friend, or join a group room, the people you are linked with see your display name and the titles you both matched on. They never see your email address, your date of birth, or your location. What you sync between your own devices (Flix) is visible only to you.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted third parties to operate the app. They process data only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and TV metadata.
  • MovieGlu — cinema and showtime data (an approximate location is sent to retrieve nearby showtimes).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing.
  • Expo push notification service — delivery of notifications.

If you add a streaming account, your device connects directly to the streaming server you entered in order to list and play titles. We do not receive, relay or store that traffic, and we hold your credentials only for a playlist you added with “Add from the web”. That service has its own privacy practices.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. Usage data (optional)

If you accept, we record — with the date, and tied to your account — when you open the app; the streaming platforms you tap (for example Netflix) and for which title; the titles you like, pass on or mark as seen, and your rating; the film, series and people pages you open; and what you search for.

In Flix we also record what you start watching (the channel, film or episode title) and how long you stay in the player. We never record stream addresses, your server or your Flix login.

We use it to understand how the app is used and to improve it — for example to see which platforms and titles are popular. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics companies: it stays in our own database.

You are asked once, the first time you open the app, and nothing is recorded before you answer. Accept and Decline carry the same weight. You can change your mind at any time in Profile → Usage data. If you decline or withdraw, we stop recording and stop using what was already recorded; all we add from then on is a record of your choice. What was already recorded stays until you delete it (see below) or it expires.

We never record the content of your messages, your location, your contacts or your streaming account details.

Usage data is deleted automatically after 24 months. “Delete my usage data” (Profile → Usage data) deletes it immediately, and deleting your account deletes it too.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, likes, matches, and links are deleted from our systems, subject to short backup windows and any records we must keep for legal reasons. Usage data follows its own, shorter schedule (see "Usage data" above).

What Flix syncs between your devices (resume points, TV favourites, reminders, recently watched, settings) stays on your account until you erase it or delete your account: when you turn off Flix’s “Sync between devices” setting, the app offers to erase what is already on your account (turn it off on your other devices too, or they would send it again). A reminder whose time has passed is removed from your account the next time your reminders change.

7. Your rights

You can access and update your profile in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete account), from our Delete Account page, or by emailing us. We answer requests within 30 days.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the country you live in. Where required, those transfers rely on the safeguards offered by the provider (such as standard contractual clauses).

9. Children

FlickAlong is not directed to children. You must meet the minimum age required in your country to create an account. We use your date of birth to restrict access to age-inappropriate film and series content.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of authentication tokens and any streaming-account credentials you type in the app in your device's Keychain, encrypted transmission, and per-user database access rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.

12. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, email info@digi4all.ai.
