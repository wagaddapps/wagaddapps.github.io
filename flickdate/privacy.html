This Privacy Policy explains how FlickDate ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. FlickDate is a dating app for adults: you must be 18 or older to use it. By using FlickDate you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (stored securely in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Profile information: your display name, date of birth, gender, who you are interested in meeting, your country/region, an optional bio, and the photos you upload. Your date of birth is used to enforce the 18+ requirement and to apply age filters you and others set.
  • Activity in the app: the titles you like or pass on, the people you like or pass on, your matches, and the taste profile derived from your activity to power recommendations and matching.
  • Usage data, only if you accept it: when you open the app, the streaming platforms you tap, the titles you like, pass on or mark as seen, the pages you open, and what you search for.
  • Messages and date proposals: the messages you send and receive in a match, and any cinema-date proposal you create. They are stored so the conversation works across your devices, and are screened automatically for content our rules prohibit.
  • Location, only with your permission: precise coordinates, used to compute the distance between you and other users and to find cinemas near you. Your exact position is never shown to anyone — other users only ever see an approximate distance. If you decline, discovery falls back to your selected country's main city and you can still use the app.
  • Safety data: the blocks and reports you make, and reports made about you, together with the content reported.
  • Device & notification data: a push notification token so we can send you notifications, and basic technical information needed to operate the app.
  • Subscription status: whether you hold an active subscription. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To provide the core features: discovery, liking, matching, messaging, and cinema-date planning.
  • To show how far away someone is, and to show streaming availability and cinema showtimes near you.
  • To send notifications you have enabled (new matches, new messages).
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, if you accept usage data.
  • To keep people safe: enforcing the 18+ rule, screening messages, and acting on reports and blocks.
  • To manage subscriptions and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. What other users can see

The people your profile is shown to can see your photos, display name, age, gender, bio, and an approximate distance from them. They cannot see your email address, your date of birth, or your exact location. You are only shown to others while discovery is switched on in your profile, and accounts under 18 are never discoverable.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted third parties to operate the app. They process data only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage of your profile photos.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and TV metadata.
  • MovieGlu — cinema and showtime data (an approximate location is sent to retrieve nearby showtimes).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing.
  • Expo push notification service — delivery of notifications.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to investigate a serious safety issue.

5. Usage data (optional)

If you accept, we record — with the date, and tied to your account — when you open the app; the streaming platforms you tap (for example Netflix) and for which title; the titles you like, pass on or mark as seen, and your rating; the film, series and people pages you open; and what you search for.

We use it to understand how the app is used and to improve it — for example to see which platforms and titles are popular. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics companies: it stays in our own database.

You are asked once, the first time you open the app, and nothing is recorded before you answer. Accept and Decline carry the same weight. You can change your mind at any time in Profile → Usage data. If you decline or withdraw, we stop recording and stop using what was already recorded; all we add from then on is a record of your choice. What was already recorded stays until you delete it (see below) or it expires.

We never record the content of your messages, your location, your contacts or your streaming account details.

Usage data is deleted automatically after 24 months. “Delete my usage data” (Profile → Usage data) deletes it immediately, and deleting your account deletes it too.

6. Safety, reporting and moderation

Every profile and every conversation has a report and a block action. Reporting someone also blocks them, so they cannot contact you or reappear in your discovery deck. Reports are reviewed and acted on — content removed, and the account suspended or terminated where warranted — normally within 24 hours. Messages are additionally screened automatically for prohibited content. To reach us about safety at any time, email info@digi4all.ai.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, likes, matches, and messages are deleted from our systems, subject to short backup windows and to the limited records we must keep for legal or safety reasons — for example a record of a report made against you, so that a banned user cannot simply return. Usage data follows its own, shorter schedule (see "Usage data" above).

8. Your rights

You can access and update your profile in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete account), from our Delete Account page, or by emailing us. We answer requests within 30 days.

9. International transfers

FlickDate is available in many countries, and our providers may process data outside the country you live in. Where required, those transfers rely on the safeguards offered by the provider (such as standard contractual clauses).

10. Age requirement

FlickDate is strictly for adults aged 18 or over. We collect your date of birth at sign-up, our servers refuse to make an under-18 account discoverable, and accounts found to belong to minors are removed. The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a minor is using the app, report the profile in-app or email info@digi4all.ai.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of authentication tokens on your device, encrypted transmission, and per-user database access rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.

13. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, email info@digi4all.ai.
