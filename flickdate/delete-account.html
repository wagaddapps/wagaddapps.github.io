Delete Your Account

FlickDate

You can permanently delete your FlickDate account and associated data at any time.

In the app (recommended)

  • Open FlickDate and sign in.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and personal data are removed immediately.

By email

If you can't access the app, email info@digi4all.ai from the email address linked to your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials.
  • Your profile (name, date of birth, gender, country).
  • Your activity: likes, passes, matches, and partner/friend links.
  • Your push notification token.

What may be retained

Subscription and transaction records held by Apple or Google are governed by their policies. We may retain limited records where required by law.

Questions? Contact info@digi4all.ai.
